# Homepage artwork

Both images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the Vila Zizel design proposal. The PNGs are the original artwork, and the WebP files are the prototype exports.

## Ionic capital & olive

`assets/ionic-capital-original.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: hero artwork for the Vila Zizel Greek guesthouse's editorial website, a wide landscape 3:2 photograph-style still life. Art direction: museum-grade modern hospitality campaign, physically believable shot on medium-format film. On the RIGHT THIRD most of a monumental carved pale warm limestone Ionic capital and upper fluted column enters from below, cropped intentionally by the right edge. The elegant volute catches a strong diagonal shaft of late-afternoon Messenian sun from upper right. A real slender olive sprig with silvery olive leaves intrudes from the upper right corner and casts a sparse shadow onto the stone. Background is weathered very dark almost black olive plaster, especially the entire LEFT HALF, with velvety low-contrast shadow and no objects there, so Ivory HTML headlines can be set there. Entire composition restrained and tactile, creamy stone, bronze warmth, dense shadow, subtle grain, emotionally transporting, absolutely avoid blue sky, views of buildings, staged hotel amenities, drapes, statues, extra columns, rendered gold, people, typography, frames, watermarks. This is a botanical/architectural close-up editorial still life, not a photo of a real property. Make column and olive leaves sufficiently bright and sharp, shadow dark but not black crush. The key architectural details fit in the rightmost 46 percent so a mobile right-aligned crop is possible.

## Olive & sea

`assets/olive-and-sea-original.png`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: Vila Zizel homepage destination editorial still life, landscape 3:2 image. A gnarled old Greek olive branch with a small cluster of real silver-green olive leaves enters from the upper left into a close view of warm oyster-colored limewashed wall. Late afternoon sunlight makes a beautiful softly diffused shadow of extra olive foliage, diagonally across the wall. In the lower right, beyond the low rough wall, a soft subtle sliver of distant blue-gray sea catches light, with a muted rugged shoreline of the southern Peloponnese in the background. Quiet material and sense of place, shot on medium-format editorial film. Palette limestone, green-black, straw and muted sea ink. Beautiful restraint, softly granular without being hazy, rich natural contrast. The olive and the edge of the wall are in focus. An atmospheric conceptual landscape, no photograph of the accommodation, no resort features, no furniture, no people, no classical column, no words, no typography, no symbols, no watermark. Keep details readable within center 70 percent for portrait cropping.
